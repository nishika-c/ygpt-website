Our Story

About YGPT

Founded by Global Humanitarian and Transformation Pioneer, Maitreya Dadashreeji, YGPT is a vibrant community of like-minded youth — students and professionals from diverse backgrounds, united by a shared commitment to humanity, peace, and Mother Earth.

Our mission is to build the emotional intelligence and resilience of youth through transformative development sessions, selfless acts of service, and green environmental initiatives. YGPT provides a welcoming, judgement-free, friendly space filled with positivity, genuine connections, and support.

Since our founding in 2013, we have touched over 1 Lakh lives across India — through 13 clubs, 40+ institutes, and a growing community of changemakers who believe that inner transformation is the foundation of global peace.

Mission

Our aim is to build on the emotional intelligence and resilience of the youth through transformative youth development sessions, selfless acts of service, and green environmental initiatives. YGPT provides a welcoming, judgement-free, friendly space filled with positivity, genuine connections, and support. Our Core Values

Inner Transformation

We believe lasting change in the world begins within. Our seminars, mentoring, and the Youth Bodh workshop help youth discover clarity, purpose, and emotional resilience.

Selfless Service

From blood donation camps and food drives to visiting orphanages and old age homes — we act on our belief that small, consistent acts of kindness build a compassionate society.

Environmental Action

Through tree plantation drives, clean-up campaigns, and Valuing Mother Earth seminars, we are nurturing a generation of green champions committed to healing our planet.

Inclusivity

YGPT is a judgement-free space. We welcome youth from all backgrounds, beliefs, and cities — united by a shared love for humanity and a desire to make a difference.

Community

With 13 clubs across India — from Mumbai and Pune to Delhi, Hyderabad, and Punjab — we are building a network of changemakers who support and inspire each other.

Youth Leadership

Every event, every drive, every seminar is youth-led. We don't just talk about empowering youth — we give them the tools, mentorship, and platform to lead.

Our Founder

Maitreya Dadashreeji

Enabling the Youth; Transforming the World!

YGPT was founded under the guidance of Maitreya Dadashreeji — a Global Humanitarian, Transformation Pioneer, and the heart behind MaitriBodh Parivaar. His vision of a Chinta Mukt Bharat — a stress-free, worry-free India — is the driving force behind everything YGPT does.

Maitreya Dadashreeji believes that true transformation begins within every individual. When youth transform from the inside out — finding inner peace, emotional strength, and a sense of purpose — they naturally become agents of positive change in the world around them.

Discovering the Vision

During his journey of self-reflection and personal growth, Maitreya Dadashreeji discovered that simply by serving society, individuals could immediately establish a connection with their hearts, paving the way for inner transformation and growth.

In his twenties, Maitreya Dadashreeji selflessly served society by donating food and clothing. As a doctor, he also arranged free healthcare camps, providing support to patients through blood donations and offering treatment and medication at no cost.

Recognising that youth — especially in the age group of 16 to 30 — are untapped powerhouses of energy, Maitreya Dadashreeji understood that if channelised correctly, youth can transform the world to become a better place. To foster youth transformation, he initiated 'Youth Force for Better India' with just 11 volunteers to extend the impact of doing good in the society. He conducted youth seminars, became their friend and provided guidance to help discover their highest potential.
