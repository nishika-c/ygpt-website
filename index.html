About YGPT

Founded by Global Humanitarian and Transformation Pioneer, Maitreya Dadashreeji, YGPT is a vibrant community of like-minded youth — students and professionals from diverse backgrounds, united by a shared commitment to humanity, peace, and Mother Earth.

Our mission is to build the emotional intelligence and resilience of youth through transformative development sessions, selfless acts of service, and green environmental initiatives. YGPT provides a welcoming, judgement-free, friendly space filled with positivity, genuine connections, and support.
